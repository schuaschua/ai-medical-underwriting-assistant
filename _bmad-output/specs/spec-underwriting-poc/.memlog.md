- (direction) Source: party-mode brainstorm with Darrel 2026-10-06 (thread). Darrel approved writing the spec before Da Vinci's architecture principles round; no docs/architecture/architecture.md exists yet.
- (decision) Scope is a POC proving an LLM can classify, extract, and compare medical evidence against underwriting guidelines via RAG, auditably. Synthetic data only.
- (capability) CAP-1 Upload: a customer uploads a PDF and sees per-page progress.
- (capability) CAP-2 Classification gate per page: medical vs non-medical with a confidence score, before any extraction.
- (decision) Gate routing: >=90% medical proceeds to extraction; >=90% non-medical asks uploader to discard or keep (keep goes to underwriter queue); <90% either way goes to underwriter triage queue to accept (into extraction) or deny (discard). Confirmed by Darrel.
- (capability) CAP-3 Underwriter triage queue: underwriter sees thumbnail, predicted type, confidence, AI reason; accepts or denies.
- (capability) CAP-4 Extraction: facts extracted with page number and verbatim quote; quote verified in code to exist on that page, unverified quotes flagged.
- (capability) CAP-5 Guideline retrieval (RAG): each extracted fact retrieves matching rules from the synthetic manual; results carry rule_id.
- (capability) CAP-6 Verdict: AI suggests standard, loaded premium (with loading), decline, or refer to underwriter; each reason cites fact pages and rule_ids. Labelled AI suggestion, not a decision.
- (decision) Fourth verdict 'refer to underwriter' added for low confidence. Confirmed by Darrel.
- (capability) CAP-7 Auditable result view: PDF beside findings; clicking a citation jumps to and highlights the source line; reasons link to manual rules.
- (capability) CAP-8 Retrieval bake-off: pgvector vs Azure AI Search behind one retriever interface, same chunks/embeddings/LLM; UI Compare toggle shows both results side by side; scoreboard over the case set.
- (capability) CAP-9 Classifier bake-off: LLM classifier vs Azure AI Document Intelligence custom classifier behind one classifier interface on the same labelled page set; scoreboard.
- (capability) CAP-10 Audit log: every classification, override, accept/deny, extraction and verdict recorded with actor and timestamp.
- (capability) CAP-11 Synthetic data: ~200-page underwriting manual generated from a structured rule table (~40 impairments, thresholds, debits, rule_ids) grounded in public ADA/AHA/WHO guidance; ~20 synthetic medical case PDFs with expected rules and verdicts; negative non-medical pages (invoice, payslip, passport, recipe, utility bill) and edge pages (blank, rotated, handwritten note, mixed file).
- (decision) Manual is synthetic because real manuals (Swiss Re Life Guide, Munich Re MIRA) are proprietary. Confirmed by Darrel.
- (decision) Cloud is Azure: org standards docs/standards/azure.md (Container Apps, PostgreSQL Flexible, Microsoft Foundry, Terraform). Azure AI Search and Document Intelligence are outside the org standard and need architecture decisions.
- (constraint) Synthetic data only; no real personal or health data enters any environment.
- (constraint) Human stays in the loop: AI never issues a final decision; result screen states 'AI suggestion, not a decision'.
- (constraint) Every displayed fact and reason must be traceable to a document page or manual rule_id; uncited claims are not shown as findings.
- (constraint) Bake-off fairness: contenders share inputs, chunking, embedding model and LLM; only the component under test differs.
- (constraint) Follow org standards in docs/standards/ (Azure, Terraform, security, coding style); exceptions recorded.
- (decision) Scoreboard metrics. Retrieval: correct rule_ids in top 5, verdict accuracy, latency, monthly cost, setup effort. Classifier: accuracy, calibration (are >=90% scores right >=90%), queue rate, cost per page.
- (assumption) Two roles: customer (uploader) and underwriter.
- (assumption) Per-page classification, as proposed by the room; Darrel did not object.
- (question) Which LLM and embedding model on Foundry (Azure OpenAI or Claude via Foundry)?
- (question) Does the customer uploader sign in (org standard requires Entra sign-in on every route), or is a demo account enough?

- (question) Who is the demo audience, and is there a deadline?
